# Duck Hunt - Player Guide

Welcome to Duck Hunt! This guide will help you master the hand gesture controls and become a skilled duck hunter.

## 🎯 Getting Started

### Starting the Game
1. Make sure you have a webcam connected
2. Launch the game from the CVGames App Store
3. The game will open in a new window
4. You'll see a sky background with grass at the bottom

## 🤲 Hand Gesture Controls

### Gun Gesture
- **Extend your index finger** (pointing finger)
- **Keep other fingers closed** (middle, ring, and pinky)
- **Thumb can be in any position** for aiming
- This creates the "gun" gesture for aiming

### Aiming
- **Move your hand** around the screen to aim
- **Blue cursor** shows your gun position
- **Point at ducks** to line up your shot
- **Keep the gesture steady** for accurate aiming
- **Cursor stays visible** even with slight hand movements
- **Stabilized cursor** reduces shaking when hand is stationary
- **Aim lock** prevents cursor movement during shooting gesture
- **Smooth tracking** for better precision

### Shooting
- **Maintain the gun gesture** (index finger extended)
- **Move your thumb in a quarter-circle motion**: from left to top
- **Gesture is very forgiving**: Only 20% of quarter-circle motion required
- **Wide time window**: 0.05-2.0 seconds for gesture completion
- **Minimal movement**: Very small thumb motion is sufficient
- **Aim automatically locks** during shooting gesture to prevent drift
- **Yellow flash** indicates a shot was fired
- **Cooldown period** of 0.3 seconds between shots

## 🦆 Game Mechanics

### Duck Behavior
- **Ducks spawn** from the bottom of the screen
- **Random trajectories** - each duck flies in a different path
- **Flapping animation** - wings move realistically
- **Multiple ducks** can be on screen at once (up to 3)

### Scoring System
- **10 points** for each duck hit
- **Score display** in top-left corner
- **Ducks shot counter** shows your accuracy
- **Timer** shows remaining time (60 seconds total)

### Game Flow
1. **Ducks appear** every 2 seconds
2. **Aim and shoot** using hand gestures
3. **Hit effects** show successful shots
4. **Game ends** after 60 seconds
5. **Final score** is displayed

## 🎮 Tips for Success

### Hand Positioning
- **Good lighting** is essential for accurate hand tracking
- **Stay within camera view** for reliable detection
- **Use your dominant hand** for better control
- **Keep hand steady** when aiming

### Shooting Strategy
- **Lead your shots** - ducks are moving targets
- **Aim for the center** of the duck for best hit chance
- **Watch duck patterns** to predict movement
- **Don't waste shots** - there's a cooldown period

### Performance Tips
- **Practice the gun gesture** before starting
- **Find comfortable hand position** for extended play
- **Take breaks** if hand gets tired
- **Adjust lighting** if tracking is poor

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Hand Not Detected
- **Check lighting** - ensure good illumination
- **Move closer** to the camera
- **Clean camera lens** if needed
- **Restart game** if problems persist

### Gesture Not Working
- **Practice gun gesture** - index finger only extended
- **Check finger positions** - others should be mostly closed
- **Adjust hand angle** for better detection
- **Try different hand positions**
- **For shooting**: Practice the quarter-circle thumb motion from left to top
- **Gesture is very forgiving** - small movements are sufficient
- **Wide time window** - take your time with the motion

### Poor Performance
- **Close other applications** to free up resources
- **Check camera quality** and settings
- **Ensure stable internet** if using cloud processing
- **Restart computer** if issues continue

### Button Not Working
- **Ensure shooting gesture** is complete (quarter-circle motion)
- **Point directly at button** center for best detection
- **Hold gesture steady** when pointing at button
- **Try multiple attempts** if button doesn't respond
- **Check hand position** - make sure hand is clearly visible

## 🎯 Advanced Techniques

### Quarter-Circle Shooting Technique
- **Start with thumb extended to the left** (like pointing left)
- **Smoothly move thumb in an arc** from left to top
- **Very forgiving motion**: Only 20% of quarter-circle is required
- **Wide time window**: 0.05-2.0 seconds for completion
- **Minimal movement needed**: Small thumb motion is sufficient
- **Visual hint appears** when aiming to show the correct motion
- **Gesture is much easier** than before - no need for perfect quarter-circle

### Quick Shooting
- **Pre-aim** at common duck spawn areas
- **React quickly** to new duck appearances
- **Use peripheral vision** to spot new targets
- **Develop muscle memory** for hand positioning

### Accuracy Improvement
- **Practice steady aiming** without shooting
- **Learn duck movement patterns**
- **Adjust for shot delay** and cooldown
- **Focus on precision** over speed initially

## 🎯 Game Rules & Objectives

### Win Condition
- **Shoot at least 50% of all ducks** to win the game
- **Progress is shown** in real-time during gameplay
- **Color-coded progress**: Red (below 30%), Yellow (30-50%), Green (50%+)

### Game Completion
- **Game ends** when all ducks are gone (shot or escaped)
- **Win screen** appears if you hit 50% or more ducks
- **Game over screen** appears if you hit less than 50% ducks
- **Final score** and statistics are displayed

### Button Interactions
- **All clickable elements** use the same shooting gesture
- **Point and shoot** at buttons to interact
- **Replay button**: Restart the game after completion
- **Back button**: Return to app store (top-left corner)

## 🚪 Exiting the Game

### Back Button
- **Top-left corner** of the screen
- **Hover and pinch** index and middle fingers
- **Approval dialog** will appear
- **Confirm exit** to return to app store

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- **Press 'B'** to open exit dialog
- **Press 'R'** to restart game
- **Press 'Q'** or 'ESC' to quit immediately
