# 💪 Fitness Trainer

Get real-time feedback on your exercise form using pose estimation.

## Category
Fitness

## Age Range
12+

## Difficulty
Medium

## Duration
10-30 min

## Requirements
- OpenCV
- MediaPipe
- Python 3.7+
- Webcam

## Features
- Exercise form
- Workout guides
- Real-time feedback

## How to Play
1. Run:
   ```bash
   python __init__.py
   ```
2. Play:
   - Follow the workout guides and perform exercises in front of your webcam.
   - Receive real-time feedback on your form and technique.
   - Improve your fitness and track your progress.
3. Quit: Press `q`.
