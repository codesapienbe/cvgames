# CVGames App Store - Player Guide

Welcome to the CVGames App Store! This guide will help you navigate and use the app store with simple hand gestures.

## 🎮 Getting Started

### Starting the App Store
1. Make sure you have a webcam connected
2. Run the app store: `uv run appstore`
3. The app store will open in fullscreen mode
4. You'll see a grid of game cards

## 🤲 Hand Gesture Controls

### Basic Navigation
- **Move your hand** around the screen to see the cursor
- **Hover over a game card** to highlight it (card will zoom in)
- **Keep your hand steady** over a card to start selection (green ring appears)

### Game Selection
- **Keep your hand steady** over a game card for 1 second (progress ring will fill)
- **Close your fist** (all fingers to palm) when the card turns green and shows "READY!"
- The game will launch automatically when selected
- **Visual feedback**: Cards change color from blue (hovering) to green (ready to select)

### Pausing Interaction
- **Raise both hands** (palms facing camera) to pause gesture detection
- This is useful when you need to move around or do other activities
- **Lower your hands** after 2 seconds to resume interaction
- The status will show "Interaction: ENABLED" when active

## 📱 Interface Elements

### Game Cards
- **Large blue rectangles** with game information
- **Game name** at the top
- **Description** at the bottom
- **Icon placeholder** in the center
- **Zoom effect** when you hover over them

### Visual Feedback
- **Dark blue cards**: Normal state
- **Blue cards with zoom**: Your hand is hovering over them
- **Green cards**: Ready to select (after 1 second hold)
- **Progress ring with percentage**: Shows selection progress (0% to 100%)
- **"READY!" text**: Appears when card is ready for selection
- **INFO button**: Bottom left corner with help instructions
- **Status text**: Shows if interaction is enabled or disabled

### Navigation
- **Left/Right arrows**: Navigate between pages (if you have more than 6 games)
- **Page indicator**: Shows current page and total pages
- **INFO button**: Bottom left corner - hover and open palm to see help
- **Instructions**: Always visible at the bottom of the screen

## 🎯 Tips for Best Experience

### Hand Positioning
- **Keep your hand open** when navigating (fingers spread)
- **Use your dominant hand** for better control
- **Stay within camera view** for reliable tracking
- **Maintain good lighting** for accurate hand detection

### Game Selection
- **Hover first**: Move your hand over the game you want
- **Wait for zoom**: The card should zoom in when you hover
- **Watch the ring**: Wait for the green progress ring to appear
- **Close fist**: Only close your fist when the ring is visible

### Troubleshooting
- **If gestures don't work**: Check if interaction is enabled (green status)
- **If hand tracking is poor**: Improve lighting or move closer to camera
- **If games don't launch**: Make sure you're closing your fist completely
- **To quit**: Press 'q' on your keyboard

## 🔄 Game Lifecycle

### Launching Games
1. Hover over a game card
2. Wait for the green progress ring
3. Close your fist to launch
4. The game will start in fullscreen
5. Play the game using its specific controls

### Returning to App Store
- **Back Button**: All games now have a standardized BACK button in the top-left corner
- **Approval Dialog**: Clicking the back button shows a confirmation dialog
- **Gesture Control**: Pinch index and middle fingers over the BACK button
- **Keyboard Shortcut**: Press 'B' to open the approval dialog
- **Auto-Timeout**: Dialog closes automatically after 5 seconds if no action taken
- **Always**: The app store will be waiting when you exit a game

### Switching Between Games
1. Return to app store from current game
2. Navigate to a different game card
3. Launch the new game using the same process
4. No need to restart the app store
