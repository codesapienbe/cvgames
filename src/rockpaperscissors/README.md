# 🪨 Rock Paper Scissors AI

Play classic Rock Paper Scissors against AI using hand gestures.

## Description
Play the classic Rock Paper Scissors game against an AI opponent using your hand gestures, detected by your webcam. The AI recognizes your gesture and instantly responds with its own choice. Track your wins, losses, draws, and streaks.

## Category
Hand Tracking

## Age Range
3+

## Difficulty
Easy

## Duration
5-15 min

## Requirements
- OpenCV
- MediaPipe
- Python 3.7+
- Webcam

## Features
- Gesture recognition
- AI opponent
- Score tracking

## How to Play
1. Run:
   ```bash
   python __init__.py
   ```
2. Play:
   - Show your hand to the camera and form one of the three gestures:
     - Rock: Fist (no fingers extended)
     - Paper: Open hand (all fingers extended)
     - Scissors: Two fingers extended (index and middle)
   - The AI will detect your gesture and play against you.
   - Results and scores are displayed on the screen, including win/loss/draw streaks.
3. Quit: Press `q` to exit.
