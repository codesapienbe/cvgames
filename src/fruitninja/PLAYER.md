# 🎯 Fruit Ninja CV - Player Guide

## 🎮 How to Play

### Controls
- **Index Finger**: Your sword - use it to slice fruits and avoid bombs
- **Hand Movement**: Move your hand to control the sword
- **'q' Key**: Quit the game
- **'r' Key**: Restart game (when game over)

### Game Rules
1. **Slice Fruits**: Use your index finger to slice flying fruits
   - Each fruit sliced = +10 points
   - 5 different colored fruits: Red Apple, Orange, Yellow Banana, Purple Grape, Dark Red Strawberry

2. **Avoid Bombs**: Don't touch the bombs with your sword
   - Each bomb exploded = -20 points
   - 3 bombs exploded = Game Over

3. **Scoring System**
   - Fruits: +10 points each
   - Bombs: -20 points each
   - Goal: Get the highest score possible!

### Visual Feedback
- **Green Sword Trail**: Shows your sword movement
- **Hand Landmarks**: Displays your hand tracking
- **Fruit Slicing**: Fruits split into two halves when sliced
- **Bomb Explosion**: Red expanding circle when bomb explodes
- **Score Display**: Real-time score and bomb counter

### Tips for Success
1. **Stay Calm**: Smooth hand movements work better than frantic swiping
2. **Watch for Bombs**: Bombs spawn every 3 seconds, fruits every 1.5 seconds
3. **Use the Full Screen**: Fruits and bombs can appear anywhere
4. **Practice Timing**: Learn the arc of the fruits to slice them efficiently

### Technical Requirements
- Webcam with good lighting
- Hand visible to camera
- Python with OpenCV and MediaPipe installed

### Troubleshooting
- **Hand not detected**: Ensure good lighting and hand is clearly visible
- **Poor tracking**: Keep hand steady and avoid rapid movements
- **Game lag**: Close other applications to free up resources

Enjoy slicing fruits and achieving high scores! 🍎🍊🍌🍇🍓
