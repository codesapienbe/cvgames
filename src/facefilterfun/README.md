# 🎭 Face Filter Fun

Apply fun filters to your face in real-time.

## Category
Face Detection

## Age Range
3+

## Difficulty
Easy

## Duration
10-30 min

## Requirements
- OpenCV
- MediaPipe
- Python 3.7+
- Webcam

## Features
- Face filters
- Photo effects
- Video effects

## How to Play
1. Run:
   ```bash
   python __init__.py
   ```
2. Play:
   - Use your webcam to see your face with fun filters applied in real-time.
   - Try different photo and video effects.
   - Take screenshots or record videos with your favorite filters.
3. Quit: Press `q`.
